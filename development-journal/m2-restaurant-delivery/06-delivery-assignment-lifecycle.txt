2. HOW IT WORKS
- Discovery: Available pool returns unassigned orders in Accepted, Preparing, or Ready for Pickup statuses.
- Order Claiming: Atomically binds order.deliveryPartnerId and partner.activeOrderId, toggling partner offline.
- Package Pickup: Enforces that orders must reach Ready for Pickup before the assigned courier can mark Picked Up.
- In-Transit & Handover: Enforces Picked Up -> Out for Delivery -> Delivered canonical progression.
- Delivery Completion: Clears partner.activeOrderId, increments totalDeliveries once, and restores availability.

3. TECHNOLOGIES & CONCEPTS
- Multi-Resource Concurrency Coordination: Sorting collection locks alphabetically prevents deadlocks during multi-write units.
- Optimistic Verification with Check-and-Set: Re-evaluating eligibility inside mutex prevents double-claiming race conditions.
- Defensive Privacy Sanitation: Unassigned available orders omit customer personal phone numbers until claimed.
- Crash Reconciliation: On startup or query, partner state is reconciled with orders to self-heal orphan active IDs.

6. VIVA QUESTIONS & ANSWERS
Q1: Why cannot two independent atomic file writes guarantee consistency across collections without coordination?
A1: If the server terminates after writing orders.json but before delivery_partners.json, the order appears claimed
    while the partner remains idle. We serialize operations with _serializeMulti and use self-healing reconciliation.
Q2: What prevents deadlocks when multiple asynchronous requests lock both orders and delivery_partners?
A2: We sort the collection names alphabetically before acquiring locks. Every concurrent process acquires
    delivery_partners then orders in the exact same sequence, eliminating cyclic lock dependency.
Q3: Why can a delivery partner not claim an order while their status is offline (isAvailable: false)?
A3: To maintain platform dispatch integrity. Partners must actively declare themselves ready for duty before receiving
    assignments, preventing dispatch to inactive or absent couriers.
Q4: What happens if an assigned delivery partner attempts pickup while the order is still in 'Preparing'?
A4: The API returns HTTP 400 INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION. Food preparation cannot be bypassed; handover requires the
    kitchen to mark the order 'Ready for Pickup'.
